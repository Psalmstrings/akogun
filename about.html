OUR STORY & PURPOSE

Precision In Every Measurement. Security In Every Title.

Founded under the leadership of registered surveying professional Surv. Adediji Babatunde, AKOGUN SEC was established to bridge critical gaps in Nigerian land administration, physical development, and environmental sustainability.

We combine modern geomatics technology—including high-precision Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) GNSS, digital Total Stations, and Geographic Information Systems (GIS)—with deep legal and administrative understanding of state land registries, particularly in Lagos State and throughout Nigeria.

From individual property buyers seeking title security to institutional developers embarking on multi-hectare estate layouts, our firm delivers reliable data, clarity, and peace of mind.

LEADERSHIP & CREDENTIALS

Meet the Founder: Surv. Adediji Babatunde

Surv. Adediji Babatunde is a highly respected Registered Surveyor, Property Consultant, and Construction Professional with over a decade of hands-on experience in Nigeria's real estate, geomatics, and infrastructure sectors.

He is an alumnus of the prestigious Federal School of Surveying, Oyo, the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA), and holds an advanced Master's Degree in Housing Development and Management from the University of Ibadan.

PROFESSIONAL EXCELLENCE

Leadership That Delivers Tangible Value

With comprehensive mastery of Lagos State land administration and property documentation protocols, Surv. Babatunde has guided hundreds of corporate investors, institutions, and families through title verification, layout engineering, and risk-free acquisitions.

Key Areas of Expertise:

  • Cadastral boundary surveys, beacon setting, and certified survey plan preparation
  • Lagos Lands Bureau title charting, Governor's Consent, and Certificate of Occupancy processing
  • Estate master layout design, road networking, and physical planning coordination
  • Comprehensive environmental impact assessment and terrain hydrology analysis
