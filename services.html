CLIENT QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a provisional and a registered survey plan? +

A registered survey plan is prepared, signed, and stamped by a Registered Surveyor and lodged with the State Office of the Surveyor-General with official beacon numbers. It serves as a recognized legal document for title perfection, Governor's Consent, and Court proceedings. A provisional survey is merely preliminary and cannot be used for statutory registration.

Can AKOGUN SEC chart my prospective land before I buy it in Lagos? +

Yes. Land charting is one of our primary advisory services. We chart your coordinates against the Lagos State composite digital cadastral map to determine whether the land falls under government acquisition, committed agricultural reserves, road alignments, or is free for development.

How long does it take to complete a perimeter boundary survey? +

Field observation for standard residential plots (1 to 5 plots) typically takes 1 to 2 days. Computational reduction, drafting, beacon pillar setting, and survey plan processing usually takes between 3 to 7 working days depending on state lodgement protocols.

When is an Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) mandatory? +

Under Federal and State environmental protection laws, an EIA is required for large-scale housing estates, industrial facilities, agricultural land clearing, coastal reclamation, filling stations, and any infrastructure development likely to significantly alter the natural ecosystem.
