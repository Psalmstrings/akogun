THE PERSON BEHIND THE VISION

Meet the Founder of AKOGUN SURVEYING & ENVIRONMENTAL CONSULTANCY

Every company begins with a vision. This section introduces the founder, the professional journey behind AKOGUN SEC and the principles that guide the company’s work.

Surv. Adediji Babatunde is a highly respected Registered Surveyor, Property Consultant, and Construction Professional with over a decade of experience in Nigeria's real estate and construction industry.

Read the founder’s full profile +

Founder’s story

Name: SURV. ADEDIJI BABATUNDE.

Background: He is an alumnus of the prestigious Federal School of Surveying, Oyo, the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA), and holds a Master's Degree in Housing Development and Management from the University of Ibadan.

Vision for AKOGUN SEC: With deep expertise in land surveying, property documentation, land registration, and real estate advisory, Surv. Babatunde has a strong command of Lagos land administration and property documentation processes. He is renowned for guiding individuals, investors, and organizations through the complexities of property acquisition and real estate investment in Nigeria. As a seasoned property broker and consultant, he provides strategic advice to clients on when, where, and how to invest in real estate — helping them make informed decisions aligned with their financial goals

PROFESSIONAL VALUES

Professional Leadership

Surv. Babatunde is the Founder and Principal Consultant of Akogun Surveying and Environmental Consultancy, a firm offering professional services in surveying, property documentation, and estate development.

Through his firm, he has successfully delivered:

  • Seamless land registration and property documentation services
  • Building construction and end-to-end project management
  • Estate layout design, infrastructure planning, and development consultancy
  • Property verification, title security, and investment advisory

His firm is widely recognized for its professional excellence, reliability, and unwavering integrity.
